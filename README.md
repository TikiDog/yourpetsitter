# Wags & Whiskers 🐾
### In-Home Pet Sitting & Dog Walking — Greater Sacramento Area

This is the official website for **Wags & Whiskers**, a professional in-home pet sitting and dog walking service serving the greater Sacramento region.

---

## About This Site

A single-page website built with plain HTML and CSS — no frameworks, no dependencies. Designed to be fast, mobile-friendly, and easy to maintain.

## Pages & Sections

- **Hero** — Introduction and call to action
- **Services & Pricing** — Dog sitting, cat sitting, dog walking, home add-ons, and litter box scooping
- **About** — About Sherry J. and the Wags & Whiskers story
- **FAQ** — Frequently asked questions
- **Testimonials** — Reviews from happy pet parents
- **Gallery** — Photo gallery of visits (replace placeholders with real photos)
- **Contact / Booking Form** — Questionnaire powered by Formspree

## Contact Form

The booking questionnaire is powered by [Formspree](https://formspree.io). Submissions are delivered directly to the owner's email inbox.
