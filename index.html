My family of three has been dog sitting on Rover since 2012 — with 100+ five-star reviews, zero bad reports, and zero incidents. I'm leaving Rover behind because of the unnecessary fees they charge both sitters and customers. My goal now is simple: quality, safe, secure, and affordable in-home pet care — without the middleman.

My son is grown, and I'm ready to dive in and share my love with your pet. I'm in my early 50s, I work from home, and I have the flexibility to bring my work with me and spend real, meaningful time with your animals. Your pet will never be an afterthought.

I promise to be responsible, caring, and communicative. I have pets of my own — my dogs are my family — so I truly understand what pet owners need and want. That sense of trust you're looking for? That's exactly what I'm here to provide.

FAQ

Questions? I've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know before inquiring with Wags & Whiskers.

Q

Do You Require A Meet & Greet Before The First Booking?

Yes — a meet and greet is required for all new clients, and it's always free of charge. Think of it like speed dating: we have to make sure we match each other and it's a good fit — for you, your pet, and me. I want everyone to feel comfortable before we commit!

Q

What Areas Do You Serve?

I serve the greater Sacramento region, including Citrus Heights, Roseville, Folsom, El Dorado Hills, Auburn, and surrounding communities. Not sure if I cover your area? Just reach out and ask!

Q

Are You Insured And Licensed?

I am currently in the process of obtaining my business license and pet sitter's insurance. I'll update this page as soon as that's complete. In the meantime, I bring 12+ years of verified Rover experience with a spotless record.

Q

What Happens If My Pet Has A Medical Emergency?

Your pet's safety is my top priority. I will contact you immediately and, if needed, transport your pet to your designated vet or the nearest emergency animal clinic. I ask that all clients provide vet contact information when inquiring.

Q

How Do I Receive Updates On My Pet?

You'll receive daily updates including photos so you can relax and enjoy your time away. I communicate via text or email — whichever you prefer. On occasion I've even FaceTimed with pet parents. Just ask! 😊

Q

Do You Care For Pets With Special Needs Or Medications?

Absolutely. I have experience administering diabetes injections to both cats and dogs, and I've handled diaper changes for senior pets. If your pet has special needs, let's talk — I'm here for them too.

Q

What Is Your Cancellation Policy?

Life happens — I get it! I ask for at least 48 hours notice for cancellations when possible. For extended stays or holiday bookings, a little more notice is appreciated. I'll always do my best to work with you.

Q

How Do I Pay For Services?

I accept Venmo, Zelle, and cash. Payment is due at the start of each service period. Pay cash and save $5 per service! I'll always provide a clear summary of charges upfront — no surprises.

Q

Can I Book Recurring Visits?

Yes! Recurring bookings are welcome and encouraged. Whether you need weekly check-ins or regular pet sitting, I'm happy to set up a consistent schedule that works for you and your pet.
